How to Find a Facebook Profile ID

1

From Profile URL

If the URL is facebook.com/profile.php?id=100084..., the number after id= is the exact Profile ID.

2

From Share Link

Paste a link such as facebook.com/share/... directly into the search bar. Share links are best-effort and may expire or be restricted by Facebook.

3

One-Click Download

Our server securely handles CDN CORS negotiation so clicking Download Image immediately saves the photo as a .jpg.

🛡️
Privacy & Scope Warning: This tool will work for publicly available profiles and publicly available locked profiles only. Facebook restricts completely private or deactivated accounts. For accounts with Profile Picture Guard or Locked Profile enabled, it retrieves the public preview thumbnail and applies the maximum available CDN resolution upgrade.
